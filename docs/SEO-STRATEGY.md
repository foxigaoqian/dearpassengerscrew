# Dear Passengers SEO strategy

The target is not a fixed page count. The site is built around the questions a
player asks before release and the evidence needed to answer each one.

## Search-intent layers

1. **Entity and overview**
   - Dear Passengers
   - Dear Passengers game
   - Dear Passengers Steam
   - FLEXUS developer
2. **Availability**
   - release date
   - demo and Gamescom demo
   - download and safe-download checks
   - price and preorder
3. **Multiplayer**
   - online co-op
   - maximum player count
   - solo play
   - crossplay, local co-op and split-screen
4. **Purchase readiness**
   - Windows and platform status
   - system requirements
   - supported languages
   - confirmed feature checklist
5. **Gameplay and wiki**
   - pilot role
   - cabin crew
   - flight flow
   - passengers
   - cargo and payouts
   - weather and turbulence
   - emergency handling
   - items and aircraft areas
6. **Freshness**
   - dated Steam reveal report
   - 700K, 1M and 1.5M wishlist reports
   - Gamescom and later-public-demo plan

## Editorial moat

Each page starts with a direct answer and separates:

- **Confirmed** — published by FLEXUS or an official store.
- **Attributed** — a named publication reports a direct developer statement.
- **Observed** — visible in official footage.
- **Interpreted** — practical analysis based on confirmed systems.
- **Unknown** — no reliable answer is available.

The site does not invent a release date, price, player limit, platform, item,
class or mechanic to make an article look complete.

## Internal-link model

- Homepage links directly to the primary intents and highest-use player tools;
  secondary destinations are reached through related modules, hubs and the footer.
- Release, demo, news and wiki hubs link to their supporting clusters.
- Deep articles link laterally within their cluster and across to media, status,
  policy and primary sources.
- Every indexable route is included in the sitemap and validated for a unique
  title and canonical URL.

## Update triggers

Recheck and update related pages together when any of these change:

- exact launch date or delay;
- Steam demo, Playtest or purchase button;
- price, package or preorder;
- maximum player count or lobby behavior;
- platform, crossplay or local-co-op support;
- recommended specifications or Steam Deck status;
- supported-language table;
- new official trailer, screenshot or Steam announcement.
